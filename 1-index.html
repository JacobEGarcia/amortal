About Amortal

Amortal is an independent magazine about the science and culture of life extension: the labs trying to reverse aging, the people who refuse to die on schedule, and the strange new politics of a species negotiating with its own expiration date. We take the research seriously and the hype with a grain of salt the size of a telomere.

Nothing in this issue is medical advice. It is a map of a frontier, drawn by people who want to live long enough to see what's on the other side of it.
